The honest break-even maths

Does the Sleeping Bag actually save time?

Count the whole investment: the trip to earn it, the XP paid out by the quests, and the three-minute nap needed to keep the full bonus running.

Quest chain time~1–2 hoursWowhead describes the trip as a substantial cross-Azeroth detour; recent guide estimates put it around one to two hours.
XP from the six quests10,945 XPThis XP pays back part of the time spent questing. The actual time value depends on your level and how quickly you are leveling.
Full buff+3% XPThree full minutes in the bag: three stacks of +1% XP.

1. Subtract the quest XP from the acquisition cost

Quest XP is not wasted time: it is XP you would otherwise have to earn while leveling. Convert it into equivalent leveling time using your actual pace.

Net unlock cost = quest-chain time − (10,945 ÷ your XP per hour × 60)

For illustration, if the chain takes 90 minutes:

Normal leveling paceQuest XP is worthNet unlock cost
20,000 XP/hour32.8 minutes57.2 minutes
30,000 XP/hour21.9 minutes68.1 minutes

These are examples, not a universal leveling rate. The quest chain itself awards 10,945 XP across six quests; optional quest rewards and the 12-slot bag have not been assigned a time value.

2. Pay the three-minute nap tax

Using the one-hour buff duration stated in the Wowhead guide, you need to spend three minutes resting each hour to keep all three stacks for the full leveling hour.

57 minutes leveling × 1.03 = 58.71 minutes of normal-XP value
58.71 − 60 = −1.29 minutes per hour

That means the full-buff routine does not pay for itself if those three minutes replace active leveling. The +3% bonus gives you 1.71 minutes' worth of extra XP during the 57 minutes you do play, but the nap costs 3 minutes. Net result: about 1 minute 17 seconds lost per hour, before even counting the quest-chain investment.

3. So when is it worth doing?

It makes sense when the three-minute rest overlaps with downtime you would have had anyway: waiting for a group, travel breaks, sorting bags, eating, or a planned AFK. In that case the nap has little or no opportunity cost, and the +3% is genuinely extra XP.

Buff-duration caveat: sources disagree. The linked Wowhead guide describes a one-hour buff, while Icy Veins and other current Forever references report two hours. If the in-game buff really lasts two hours, you only need one three-minute rest per two-hour cycle: 117 minutes × 1.03 = 120.51 minutes of normal-XP value, a gain of just 0.51 minutes per two hours. On that interpretation, a 57–68 minute net unlock cost (the examples above) takes roughly 224–267 hours of leveling to repay. Check the actual Well-Rested timer in game; this one detail changes the conclusion.
Horde quest route

Follow the trail across Azeroth

The questline sends you between Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms. Horde starts in The Barrens, then visits Westfall; the remaining steps are shared by both factions. Coordinates below are approximate in-game zone coordinates.

01 · Barrens

Find the first burned-out remains

Head south of Camp Taurajo to /way 46.4, 73.9. Click the Burned-Out Remains in the rubble and accept ...and that note you found.

02 · Westfall

Investigate Alexston Farmstead

Travel to the farmstead at /way 37, 50. Click the burned-out remains in the wrecked cart, turn in the note quest and accept Stepping Stones.

03 · Stonetalon

Find the abandoned camp

Head northwest from Sun Rock Retreat to the hidden camp. Click the Pocket Litter at /way 40.7, 52.4 to turn in Stepping Stones and continue the chain.

Optional

Rekindle the campfire

At the abandoned camp, the nearby fire pit is an optional interaction. Use Simple Wood and Flint and Tinder if you want the extra campfire quest; it is not required to obtain the bag.

04 · Stonetalon

Make the jump to the dirt mound

Find the Mound of Dirt a short distance northwest of the camp at /way 39.6, 49.9. Click it to complete the current step and accept the next quest.

05 · Loch Modan

Reach the Eagle Figurine on the dam

At Stonewrought Dam, go to /way 49.4, 12.9. Jump across the carved stone heads to reach the Carved Figurine, then click it to advance the chain. This is Alliance territory.

06 · Hillsbrad

Climb into Thoradin Wall

Travel to Thoradin's Wall in northwestern Arathi Highlands at /way 22.4, 24.2. Find the Messenger Bag hanging high on the wall; use the nearby climbable route/cart to reach it, then click it to advance the chain.

Reward

Claim your Cozy Sleeping Bag

At the same location, click the Hastily Rolled-Up Satchel/package beneath the Messenger Bag to finish the final quest and receive the Cozy Sleeping Bag.

Make the trip less painful

Horde time-saving notes

ROUTE TIP 01

Start in the Barrens

Don't follow an Alliance-first route. Wowhead's Horde order starts at the Barrens burned-out remains, then sends you to Westfall.

ROUTE TIP 02

Plan your flight paths

Wowhead's suggested route assumes Horde flight paths at Orgrimmar, Crossroads and Kargath. Pick up missing flight paths when practical, or adapt the route to your character.

ROUTE TIP 03

Consider waiting until 20–25

The quest chain is a long cross-continent trip with dangerous higher-level areas. Wowhead commenters report that low-level Horde characters can have a rough time; a few extra levels or an escort can make it less punishing.

Extra loot along the way

More than a sleeping bag

The chain also hands out useful supplies, so check each quest reward before leaving a stop.

01

12-slot bag

The Stepping Stones stage includes a 12-slot bag reward. Make room in your inventory before the trip.

02

Student Fodder ×5

In WoW: Forever, Student Fodder restores health. It is separate from the Sleeping Bag's experience bonus.

03

XP at each stage

The quest chain itself awards experience as you travel, making the detour worthwhile while leveling.

Use the reward well

Three-minute camp routine

1. Place the bag somewhere safe, out of combat. 2. Lie in it for three full minutes to build the maximum +3% XP buff. 3. Head back to questing, grinding or turning in quests while the buff is active. The Wowhead Forever guide says the buff lasts one hour and that you also become rested while inside the bag. The calculator's uptime field lets you model only the portion of your session when the buff is actually up.
