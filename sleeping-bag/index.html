The honest break-even maths

Does the Sleeping Bag actually save time?

Count everything: the trip to earn it, the XP paid out by the quests, the first three-minute nap, and the short refresh needed to keep the buff rolling.

Quest chain time~1–2 hoursA substantial cross-Azeroth trip. Use 90 minutes as a midpoint example, not a guaranteed completion time.
Quest-chain XP10,945 XPSix quests pay XP back toward the time investment.
XP bonus+3% XPThree stacks of +1%, affecting both kill XP and quest XP.

1. What did getting the bag really cost?

Quest XP offsets some of the time spent on the chain. Convert the 10,945 XP reward into the number of minutes it would take you to earn that XP while leveling normally. Your leveling rate changes with level, so these are examples rather than a fixed answer.

Net unlock cost = quest travel time + first 3-minute nap − (10,945 XP ÷ your XP/hour × 60)

Assuming a 90-minute quest chain:

30,000 XP/hour21.9 min69.4 min~90 hours
40,000 XP/hour16.4 min74.9 min~97 hours

Break-even uses the recurring net gain calculated below. It assumes the chain gives 10,945 XP and takes 90 minutes; a 1-hour chain repays sooner, a 2-hour chain takes longer. Other loot (including the 12-slot bag, weapon choice and consumables) is not assigned a time value.

2. The ongoing nap tax is smaller than it looks

The key mechanic: the item has a 1-hour cooldown, but the Well-Rested buff lasts 2 hours. You do the full three-minute setup once. When the item is ready again at the one-hour mark, refresh while the existing buff is still active: one minute is enough to refresh it back to the full duration. That means one minute of downtime per hour after the initial setup, not three.

59 minutes leveling × 1.03 = 60.77 minutes of normal-XP value
60.77 − 60 = +0.77 minutes saved per hour

So after the first setup, the routine earns back about 46 seconds per hour of actual leveling. The first hour is slightly negative because the initial three-minute nap leaves 57 minutes to level: 57 × 1.03 = 58.71 minutes of normal-XP value, or 1.29 minutes behind. That's why we include the first nap in the acquisition cost above.

3. Is it worth the detour?

At a 90-minute chain and 30,000 XP/hour, the quest rewards buy back about 22 minutes of leveling time. After the first nap, you still have roughly 69 minutes of net investment to recover. At 46 seconds saved per subsequent leveling hour, break-even arrives after about 90 hours of leveling.

Practical verdict: the Sleeping Bag is a long-term investment, not a quick leveling shortcut. It is much better value if you can do the one-minute refresh during natural downtime, or share a party member's bag. If you spend a full three minutes every single hour instead, the math turns negative: 57 × 1.03 = 58.71, meaning you lose about 1 minute 17 seconds per hour before paying back the quest chain.
Horde quest route

Follow the trail across Azeroth

The questline sends you between Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms. Horde starts in The Barrens, then visits Westfall; the remaining steps are shared by both factions. Coordinates below are approximate in-game zone coordinates.

01 · Barrens

Find the first burned-out remains

Head south of Camp Taurajo to /way 46.4, 73.9. Click the Burned-Out Remains in the rubble and accept ...and that note you found.

02 · Westfall

Investigate Alexston Farmstead

Travel to the farmstead at /way 37, 50. Click the burned-out remains in the wrecked cart, turn in the note quest and accept Stepping Stones.

03 · Stonetalon

Find the abandoned camp

Head northwest from Sun Rock Retreat to the hidden camp. Click the Pocket Litter at /way 40.7, 52.4 to turn in Stepping Stones and continue the chain.

Optional

Rekindle the campfire

At the abandoned camp, the nearby fire pit is an optional interaction. Use Simple Wood and Flint and Tinder if you want the extra campfire quest; it is not required to obtain the bag.

04 · Stonetalon

Make the jump to the dirt mound

Find the Mound of Dirt a short distance northwest of the camp at /way 39.6, 49.9. Click it to complete the current step and accept the next quest.

05 · Loch Modan

Reach the Eagle Figurine on the dam

At Stonewrought Dam, go to /way 49.4, 12.9. Jump across the carved stone heads to reach the Carved Figurine, then click it to advance the chain. This is Alliance territory.

06 · Hillsbrad

Climb into Thoradin Wall

Travel to Thoradin's Wall in northwestern Arathi Highlands at /way 22.4, 24.2. Find the Messenger Bag hanging high on the wall; use the nearby climbable route/cart to reach it, then click it to advance the chain.

Reward

Claim your Cozy Sleeping Bag

At the same location, click the Hastily Rolled-Up Satchel/package beneath the Messenger Bag to finish the final quest and receive the Cozy Sleeping Bag.

Make the trip less painful

Horde time-saving notes

ROUTE TIP 01

Start in the Barrens

Don't follow an Alliance-first route. Wowhead's Horde order starts at the Barrens burned-out remains, then sends you to Westfall.

ROUTE TIP 02

Plan your flight paths

Wowhead's suggested route assumes Horde flight paths at Orgrimmar, Crossroads and Kargath. Pick up missing flight paths when practical, or adapt the route to your character.

ROUTE TIP 03

Consider waiting until 20–25

The quest chain is a long cross-continent trip with dangerous higher-level areas. Wowhead commenters report that low-level Horde characters can have a rough time; a few extra levels or an escort can make it less punishing.

Extra loot along the way

More than a sleeping bag

The chain also hands out useful supplies, so check each quest reward before leaving a stop.

01

12-slot bag

The Stepping Stones stage includes a 12-slot bag reward. Make room in your inventory before the trip.

02

Student Fodder ×5

In WoW: Forever, Student Fodder restores health. It is separate from the Sleeping Bag's experience bonus.

03

XP at each stage

The quest chain itself awards experience as you travel, making the detour worthwhile while leveling.

Use the reward well

Three-minute camp routine

1. Place the bag somewhere safe, out of combat. 2. Lie in it for three full minutes to build the maximum +3% XP buff. 3. Head back to questing, grinding or turning in quests while the buff is active. The Well-Rested XP buff lasts two hours, while the bag has a one-hour cooldown. You also count as being in a rested area while lying in the bag.
