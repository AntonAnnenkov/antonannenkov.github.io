Horde quest route

Follow the trail across Azeroth

The questline sends you between Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms. For Horde, Wowhead lists The Barrens as the first starting step, then Westfall. Coordinates below are in-game zone coordinates.

01 · Barrens

Find the first burned-out remains

Head south of Camp Taurajo to /way 46, 74 (roughly 46.7, 74.3). Click the Burned-Out Remains in the rubble and accept ...and that note you found.

02 · Westfall

Investigate Alexston Farmstead

Travel to the farmstead at /way 37, 50. Click the burned-out remains in the wrecked cart, turn in the note quest and accept Stepping Stones.

03 · Stonetalon

Find the abandoned camp

Take the path northeast of Sun Rock Retreat at /way 50.9, 52.3 and follow it to the camp at /way 40.6, 52.4. Loot the Pocket Litter from the box to finish Stepping Stones, then accept Scramble.

Optional

Rekindle the campfire

At the abandoned camp, click the campfire and use Simple Wood plus Flint and Tinder from the previous quest to complete Rekindle. This step is optional to the bag chain.

04 · Stonetalon

Make the jump to the dirt mound

Climb north up the hill, then jump from the edge to the Mound of Dirt at /way 39.6, 49.8. Complete Scramble and accept Wet Job.

05 · Loch Modan

Reach the Eagle Figurine on the dam

At the Loch Modan Dam, go to /way 49.4, 12.9. Jump across the carved stone heads facing the Wetlands to reach the Eagle Figurine. Click it to finish Wet Job and accept Eagle's Fist.

06 · Hillsbrad

Climb into Thoradin Wall

At the wall between Hillsbrad Foothills and Arathi Highlands, find the cart on the Hillsbrad side at /way 87.3, 49.6. Use it to climb into the wall, then look outside to the right for the hanging Messenger Bag. Click it to complete Eagle's Fist and accept This Must Be The Place.

Reward

Claim your Cozy Sleeping Bag

Click the Hastily Rolled-Up Satchel below the Messenger Bag to finish the final quest and receive the Cozy Sleeping Bag.

Make the trip less painful

Horde time-saving notes

ROUTE TIP 01

Start in the Barrens

Don't follow an Alliance-first route. Wowhead's Horde order starts at the Barrens burned-out remains, then sends you to Westfall.

ROUTE TIP 02

Plan your flight paths

Wowhead's suggested route assumes Horde flight paths at Orgrimmar, Crossroads and Kargath. Pick up missing flight paths when practical, or adapt the route to your character.

ROUTE TIP 03

Consider waiting until 20–25

The quest chain is a long cross-continent trip with dangerous higher-level areas. Wowhead commenters report that low-level Horde characters can have a rough time; a few extra levels or an escort can make it less punishing.

Use the reward well

Three-minute camp routine

1. Place the bag somewhere safe, out of combat. 2. Lie in it for three full minutes to build the maximum +3% XP buff. 3. Head back to questing, grinding or turning in quests while the buff is active. The Wowhead Forever guide says the buff lasts one hour and that you also become rested while inside the bag. The calculator's uptime field lets you model only the portion of your session when the buff is actually up.
